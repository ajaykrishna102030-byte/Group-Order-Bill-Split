# Group Order & Bill Split: Business Requirements Document

A sample Business Analyst project for a fictional food delivery app,
**FoodDash India**. The feature lets friends and colleagues build one
shared food cart, pay only their own share, and get a single delivery.

## Business problem
Group meals are common, but ordering them is slow. One person places the
order, pays the full amount and chases friends for money later. This leads
to abandoned carts, smaller orders and payment-related support complaints.

## Goal
Increase group orders, raise average order value and cut payment disputes
by letting each person add items and pay their own share in one place.
